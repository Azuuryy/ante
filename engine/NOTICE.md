## Changes made by Ante

These patches were applied in this order before the code was vendored. Their history is kept in Git; future engine changes are made directly in these crates.

1. `bevy-uuid`: align the optional UUID dependency.
2. `play-trace`: expose the score trace from the actual play action.
3. `jokers`: add the optional Joker rules behind `ante-extra-jokers`.
4. `endless`: extend the campaign with bounded Endless.
5. `shop-tags`: add the tag and shop transitions.
6. `consumable-use`: restrict consumable use to the original eligibility rules (`consumable_use_tests.rs`).
7. `debug`: add the `debug_add_score` and `debug_next_ante` entry points (`debug_tests.rs`).
8. `joker-current`: make Joker counters and deck helpers readable for tooltips.
9. `trace-effects`: record the XMult factor and money on each score step (`trace_effect_tests.rs`).
10. `joker-rules`: align Green Joker, Castle, Obelisk, Yorick and Joker Stencil with the original.
11. `fractional-mult`: keep Mult fractional and halve The Flint's base values like the original.
12. `consumable-room`: require room for created cards from packs; add Buy & Use (`consumable_room_tests.rs`).

Changes made after vendoring:

- The action space follows the same consumable and pack rules as `gen_actions`, and the enhancement and suit Tarots, Strength, Death and the seal Spectrals clear the hand selection after use, like `G.hand:unhighlight_all()` in the original (`consumable_highlight_tests.rs`, `tests/action_space.rs`).
- Per-card Jokers act on each scored or held card, once per repetition, after the card's own effects, like `calculate_joker{individual = true}` in `evaluate_play`: new `OnCardScored` and `OnCardHeld` effects and a `JokerOnCard` score source. Converted: Greedy, Lusty, Wrathful and Gluttonous Joker, Fibonacci, Scary Face, Even Steven, Odd Todd, Scholar, Business Card, Photograph, Walkie Talkie, Smiley Face, Golden Ticket, Rough Gem, Bloodstone, Arrowhead, Onyx Agate (scored), Baron, Shoot the Moon, Reserved Parking and Raised Fist (held). Retriggers now repeat them, and Business Card, Bloodstone and Reserved Parking roll once per repetition, which changes the number of RNG draws in a hand that holds them. Raised Fist picks the last lowest-ranked held card and skips Stone cards, like the original (`per_card_joker_tests.rs`).
- Targets are chosen in the hand before a targeting card is used, like `can_use_consumeable`: the hand stays selectable while an Arcana or Spectral pack has drawn it, a pack Tarot or Spectral is taken only when the selection fits its bounds and is used at once with it, and slot consumables stay usable while a pack is open. The `TarotHand` and `SpectralHand` stages, `ApplyTarot` and `ApplySpectral` are removed; the fixed-size action space loses its two apply slots (109 to 107) and the replay log format moves to version 4. A Planet or Tarot taken from a pack now counts as the last one used for The Fool, like `set_consumeable_usage`.
- A cleared Blind keeps its reward as rows (`reward::RoundReward`: Blind, hands left, Joker dollar bonuses, tags, interest), in the order of `G.FUNCS.evaluate_round`, until cash out. Golden Joker's $4 is one of those rows, paid at cash out like `calculate_dollar_bonus`, instead of being added at round end; the Investment tag is a tag row.
- Bosses refuse hands like `Blind:debuff_hand` (`game/boss_hand.rs`): The Psychic a hand of fewer than 5 cards, The Eye a hand type already played this round, The Mouth any hand type other than the round's first (`Game.mouth_hand`, saved with a serde default). A refused hand scores 0 but still counts as played, triggers the boss's ability and pays Matador $8, like `context.debuffed_hand` (`joker/tests/boss_interactions.rs`). A Joker whose value grows during its scoring effect adds a `ScoreSource::JokerUpgrade` step before its value, which `record_step` also keeps (`game/trace_steps.rs`). The refused hand's trace starts with a `ScoreSource::NotAllowed` step, which `record_step` keeps although it changes nothing. `game/tests/boss_runs.rs` plays whole runs against these bosses with Matador, Jolly Joker and Purple seals.
- `Game::take_events` returns what the game did outside a score trace since the last call (`event.rs`): a Gold card paying at the end of the round, a Blue or Purple Seal creating a consumable, a tag gained. The adapter turns them into `RunUpdate.notes` for logs and messages. A debuffed Purple Seal creates nothing on a discard (`Card:calculate_seal`).
- Joker messages outside the score trace are `GameEvent::JokerMessage` events with a `JokerNote` (Upgrade, Reset, dollars, XMult, Mult lost), emitted per card like the original's `context.discard` (`joker/effects_2.rs`, `effects_3.rs`, `game/round.rs`). Ride the Bus and Obelisk add a `ScoreSource::JokerReset` step when they go back to their start during a hand. Faceless Joker now counts face cards (at least 3, debuffed ones excluded) instead of 5 cards of any kind, and Mail-In Rebate skips debuffed cards (`joker/tests/discard_messages.rs`).
- Credit Card, Gift Card and Perkeo (`game/money.rs`, `game/consumable_meta.rs`, `joker/tests/economy_internals.rs`): `Game.debt` (serde default) holds what is owed below $0; purchases check `spendable()` and pay with `pay()`, and `settle_debt` runs before each action so money earned anywhere repays it; the view shows `balance()`. `Game.consumable_meta` (serde default) follows the consumables by position with their extra sell value and Negative edition; `remove_consumable_at` and `move_consumable` keep it in step, Negative consumables add slots, and `consumable_sell_value` replaces the flat $1.
- Stakes (`game/stakes.rs`, `game/tests/stakes.rs`): `Game::apply_stake` after the deck, `Game.stake` (serde default 1); `blind_amount` ports `get_blind_amount` with scaling 2 and 3 (endless antes included); `blind_reward` gives the Small Blind nothing from Red Stake; `roll_stickers` draws Eternal or Perishable with one `roll_unit` and Rental with another on shop and pack Jokers; `stickers_end_of_round` charges rentals and counts down `JokerState.perish_rounds` (serde default). A disabled Joker (`Jokers::is_disabled`) is skipped by scoring, the effect registry and `count_jokers`. Eternal Jokers cannot be sold.
- Decks (`game/decks.rs`, `game/tests/decks.rs`): `Game::apply_deck` ports `Back:apply_to_run` for the 15 decks from base limits of 4 hands and 3 discards; `Game.deck_variant` (serde default Red) keeps Green's rewards (`RoundReward.discards_left` and `discard_dollars`, no interest), Ghost's `ShopContext.spectral_rate` (a fifth shop weight, 0 for other decks, so their shops draw as before), Anaglyph's Double Tag after a Boss Blind, and Plasma's `ante_scaling` of 2 and `ScoreSource::Balanced` step. Erratic draws each card's rank and suit with `roll_index`.
- Lot 3, batches 5b and 6 (`game/joker_copies.rs`, `joker/effects_8.rs`, `joker/tests/makers_batch_five_b.rs`, `copies_batch_six.rs`): Sixth Sense (after the first hand), DNA (in `before`), Certificate (after the round's first deal), Showman (`shop_context` drops the owned Jokers and consumables from the exclusions), Astronomer (`Shop.astronomer`, saved, kept in step with the held Jokers), Invisible Joker (counts rounds; `invisible_sold` duplicates a Joker with `clone_joker`, a Negative copy losing its edition), Blueprint and Brainstorm (`run_copied` looks the target up when the effect runs, restores its state afterwards and stops at a nesting depth of `Game.copy_depth`), Triboulet, and Canio (grows in `destroy_card` for a face card).
- Lot 3, batch 5a (`joker/effects_7.rs`, `game/joker_cards.rs`, `joker/tests/makers_batch_five.rs`): 8 Ball, Superposition, Seance, Vagabond, Loyalty Card, Diet Cola, Space Joker (in `before`, so the hand scores at its new level), Hallucination (on `open_booster`), Cartomancer, Riff-raff and Marble Joker (on blind select) and Burnt Joker (on the Discard action's first use, The Hook not counting). Spectral cards are drawn with `roll_index` among the non-rare ones.
- Lot 3, batch 4b (`hand/rules.rs`, `joker/tests/rules_batch_four.rs`): `HandRules` (Four Fingers, Shortcut, Smeared Joker) and `SelectHand::best_hand_with`, `flush_with`, `straight_with` port `get_flush` and `get_straight`; a straight flush scores the cards of both. `Game::hand_rules` feeds the play, the view's hand preview and the "contains" Jokers. `Game::is_suit` replaces `Card::matches_suit` in Joker and boss suit checks: a Stone card has no suit, a Wild card every suit, Smeared Joker joins a colour. This changes scores with Stone cards in a flush or under a suit boss.
- Lot 3, batch 4a (`joker/tests/rules_batch_four.rs`): Oops! All 6s (`Game::prob_roll` doubles the numerator per copy), Chaos the Clown (`Game.free_rerolls`, saved, set at cash out and when it joins or leaves; `current_reroll_cost` is 0 while one is left and the view and action space read it), Burglar (on blind select), Juggler, Drunkard, Troubadour and Merry Andy (`hand_size`, `plays_per_round`, `discards_per_round`), Splash (the scoring hand becomes every played card) and Mr. Bones (`handle_score` goes on as cleared with a blind reward of 0).
- Lot 3, batch 3 (`joker/tests/economy_batch_three.rs`): Delayed Gratification, Egg, To Do List, To the Moon, Cloud 9, Rocket and Satellite. `reward::dollar_bonus` reads the game (Cloud 9's nines, Rocket's counter, `Game.planets_used`, discards used). Interest is `min(floor($ / 5), cap) × (1 + To the Moon count)`. `JokerState.extra_value` (serde default) adds to the sell value. `SelectorValue::Hand` holds To Do List's hand, drawn with the new `RngBackend::roll_index` among the visible hands other than the current one (a secret hand once played).
- Lot 3, batch 2b (`joker/effects_6.rs`, `game/joker_hooks.rs`, `joker/tests/hooks_batch_two.rs`): Hiker, Vampire, Seltzer, Hologram, Campfire, Ceremonial Dagger and Madness. `Card.perma_bonus` (saved with a serde default) holds Hiker's chips and scores with the card, Stone cards included. `Game::jokers_before_scoring` strips Vampire's enhancements from the scoring hand before it scores; `jokers_set_blind` runs when a blind is selected (Ceremonial Dagger, Madness, which draws its victim with `pick_random_joker`); sales call `jokers_card_sold`, added playing cards `playing_cards_added`, and `wear_seltzer` runs after each hand. `Game::destroy_joker` removes a Joker and gives back a Negative edition's slot, for every destroying effect.
- Lot 3, batch 2a (`joker/effects_5.rs`, `joker/tests/scaling_batch_two.rs`): Square Joker, Runner, Throwback, Fortune Teller, Flash Card, Red Card, Constellation, Popcorn, Turtle Bean, Ramen, Lucky Cat and Glass Joker. Their value is `JokerState.counter`, moved by `Game::grow_jokers` where the event happens: a reroll, a skipped Booster Pack, a Planet or Tarot used (`Game.tarots_used`, saved), a skipped blind, Glass cards shattered after a hand, a discard (Ramen) and the end of the round (Popcorn and Turtle Bean, eaten at nothing left). A Lucky card sets `Game.lucky_trigger` when either of its rolls hits, for Lucky Cat. A per-card Joker whose counter grows now gets a `JokerUpgrade` step too. Glass cards destroyed by The Hanged Man do not count for Glass Joker yet.
- Lot 3, batch 1 (`joker/effects_4.rs`, `joker/tests/scoring_batch_one.rs`): Swashbuckler, Blackboard, Flower Pot, Seeing Double, Stuntman (`Game::hand_size` takes 2 off), Bootstraps, Gros Michel, Cavendish, Ancient Joker and The Idol. `SelectorValue::Card` holds The Idol's card. `Game::roll_card_targets` draws The Idol's card from the full deck without Stone cards and the Ancient Joker's suit among the three others, at the end of each round and, for a Joker bought since, at the start of its first round; these draws use `pick_random_card` and change later random numbers in runs that hold them. At the end of a round Gros Michel (1 in 6) and Cavendish (1 in 1000) roll for extinction ("Extinct!" or "Safe!" notes); Gros Michel's sets `Game.gros_michel_extinct`, mirrored into the Joker and pack generators, which then offer Cavendish and no longer Gros Michel, like `pool_flags`.
- End-of-round card effects follow `get_end_of_round_effect` (`game/round.rs`): a held Gold card pays $3 when the round ends instead of during each hand's scoring, and a Blue Seal makes the Planet of the last hand played (`Game.last_hand_played`, saved with a serde default, set before a boss can refuse the hand) instead of a random one. Both repeat for a Red Seal or Mime and skip debuffed cards. A seeded run that holds a Blue Seal no longer draws a random Planet, so its later draws change.
- Prices follow `Card:set_cost`: `Jokers::full_cost` adds the edition's extra cost (Foil 2, Holographic 3, Polychrome and Negative 5), `discounted` rounds `(cost + 0.5) × (100 − discount) / 100` down, and `Game::joker_sell_value` sells a Joker for half its discounted full cost, at least $1 (selling, Temperance and the view's sell price).
- The Ox, The Hook and The Tooth apply (`game/boss_hand.rs`, `game/tests/boss_play.rs`). The Ox sets money to $0 when the hand played is the most played one over the whole run, which `handle_score` records when a Boss Blind is beaten, before `advance_ante` forgets the blind (`Game.ox_hand`; a tie goes to the stronger hand). The Hook discards two random held cards when a hand is played (`Blind:press_play`), through the same discard as the Discard action (Purple seals, discard Jokers) without spending a discard, and the hand refills them; this adds two `pick_random_card` draws per hand played against The Hook, so seeded runs that meet it diverge from earlier versions. After a play or a discard the hand refills to `hand_size()` (`Game::refill_count`, like `G.FUNCS.draw_from_deck_to_hand`) rather than by the number of cards removed; this changes only the cards drawn when the hand was already short, and draws no random numbers. The Tooth costs $1 per card played, stopping at $0 since money cannot go negative. All three trigger the boss for Matador.
